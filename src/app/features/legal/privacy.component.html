Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

Controller

Armand Veress, acting as an individual. contact@premoved.com

Processed data

  • Account — username, email address, password hash, registration date, the version of the terms you accept when registering, and metadata for your encrypted vault (the settings used to authenticate you, and your encryption keys in wrapped form, which only your password or Recovery Code can unwrap).
  • Billing and purchases — for Desktop App subscriptions: the billing metadata returned by Stripe (customer and subscription identifiers, payment status, the start and end of the paid period, the date of the first payment, and refund status), the version of the terms accepted at checkout, and any acknowledgement about the right of withdrawal. The payment itself is processed by Stripe, and card details are never received or stored here.
  • Content — your collections, repertoires, saved PGN games and interface preferences. All saved files and collection names are encrypted before they are saved in the cloud. The encryption key is generated from your password or Recovery Code, and is never sent to or stored on the server.
  • Metadata — while your content and collection names are encrypted, the server can see the structural shape of your data: the number of collections, their kind (e.g., Library or Repertoire), colors, icons, type, the number of entries in each collection, and timestamps for when entries are created or updated. This is required to organize your library. The server also sees the size of your encrypted files, which is used strictly to manage storage quotas.
  • Technical — your IP address and browser user agent are processed temporarily. This happens only during sensitive actions (like registration, sign-in, or password reset) and in short-lived server logs strictly to prevent abuse, spam, and malicious attacks. Your IP address is not stored permanently, and is not used for any other purpose.
  • Sessions — for each active session where you are signed in: a short description of it (such as "Chrome on Windows"), the date of the sign in and when it was last accessed. When signing out of an active session, the data related to it is deleted immediately.

Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash and cannot be read back by anyone.

Legal basis for the processed data

  • Account, content, and metadata, to provide the service you signed up for — performance of a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Billing data, to sell and run a subscription — performance of a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Accounting and tax records are kept because the law requires it, Article 6(1)(c) GDPR, and the record that a refund was granted is kept to prevent the refund policy being used repeatedly — legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Technical data, to prevent automated abuse and keep the service running — legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Email, to confirm your account, reset your password and tell you about changes affecting your account — performance of a contract. There is no marketing email.

Analytics and cookies

There are no analytics, ads, or third-party trackers. The only cookie set is strictly necessary to keep you logged in, and it is deleted the moment you log out. Your board and interface preferences, as well as your device-specific encryption key, are saved locally in your browser. These elements are essential for the app to function, are never shared with anyone, and do not track you across other websites.

Processors

Cloudflare (delivery and protection of the site), PidginHost (the server), Resend (account email) and Stripe (payment processing for the Desktop App; Stripe is an independent controller for the payment itself and its own fraud checks). Where data leaves the European Union it travels under the standard contractual clauses those providers publish.

Retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account. An account that is never confirmed by email is deleted automatically. Furthermore, if you reset your password without your Recovery Code or an active signed-in device, your encrypted collections and saved games are immediately and permanently deleted, as they become mathematically unreadable. Deleting your account removes your username, email address, password hash, collections, saved games, sessions, immediately and irreversibly. Payment records — transaction dates, amounts and the invoice history held by Stripe — are kept for as long as accounting and tax law require, even after the account is deleted. If a refund was ever granted, a record of it also remains with no link to you: the account reference is removed and only a one-way hash of the email address is kept, so that the refund policy cannot be avoided by closing an account.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Erasure is also available immediately in Settings. Write to contact@premoved.com. You may also complain to the Romanian supervisory authority ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro) or to the authority where you live.

Children

The service is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe an account belongs to someone under 16, please contact PremovedPrep at contact@premoved.com and the account will be deleted immediately.

Changes

The date above changes when this policy does. A material change is announced by email.
